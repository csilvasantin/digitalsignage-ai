02 · The solution

One platform, 20 ways to activate the space.

Admira.tv connects screens, players and sensors so the space works as one system: create, operate and measure digital experiences in stores, offices, events and public spaces.

  • Digital signage

    Plans and publishes content across one screen or a complete network.

  • Content catalogue

    Organises creative assets and campaigns for fast reuse and distribution.

  • IoT and analytics

    Connects players and sensors, and measures aggregated attention and behaviour to improve content and spaces.

FOR screen networks, retailers, offices and events.
