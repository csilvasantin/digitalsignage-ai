01 · The challenge

Every screen can be a medium. Few are.

Physical spaces have an audience; the hard part is turning it into inventory a brand can buy.

01

Invisible inventory

In-store screens do not appear on any map an advertiser can consult.

02

Buying DOOH is opaque

Where, to whom and at what price: the brand can’t see it before committing.

03

Planning is manual

Choosing locations, dates and audience is usually done with spreadsheets and emails.

02 · The solution

Turn your network into retail media.

Admira App connects advertising with physical spaces. A network view helps you discover locations and plan where your message belongs.

  • See the full network

    Explore the location map and discover spaces. Compare networks to choose where your message belongs.

  • Shape your campaign

    Define dates, placements and target audiences. Review reach and budget estimates to prepare a request.

  • Activate new opportunities

    Connect advertisers, agencies and network owners. Expand retail media to screens, background audio and avatars.
