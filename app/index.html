01 · The challenge

Installing is the start. Maintaining is the job.

A screen network is equipment spread across many locations: someone has to install it, know what is on each site and show up when something fails.

01

What is installed, and where?

Without an inventory of locations and equipment, every intervention starts by finding out what is on site.

02

Who do I call?

The store knows something is wrong, but not whom to tell or how to describe it.

03

Untracked interventions

Requests by phone and chat that nobody can follow through to completion, with nearby installers who are not connected with whoever needs them.

02 · The solution

The whole fleet, in one place.

Admira App, with Yokup, connects stores, installers and AI agents: from registering each location and its equipment to installation, maintenance and the final review of every intervention.

  • Fleet inventory

    Register your locations and equipment. Link every incident to the store and the device affected.

  • Installation and maintenance

    Installers say where they work and manage the interventions in their area. From the first alert to the final review, one place to follow every intervention.

  • Management with people and agents

    Review open, in-progress and resolved incidents, organise priorities and combine AI agent assistance with intervention by local talent.

FOR network managers, stores and installers.

03 · How it works

Three steps, one chain.

What goes in on one side of AdmiraNeXT comes out on the other: the same data end to end.

  1. 1

    01 · INSTALL

    Register the location and its equipment; the local installer receives the mission.

  2. 2

    02 · MAINTAIN

    The store reports what is happening and which device is affected; people and agents organise the intervention.

  3. 3

    03 · MANAGE

    Resolution and the service review are recorded: the fleet stays up to date.
