01 · The challenge

The space has many systems. And no single panel.

Screens, music, scent, Wi-Fi and security usually live in separate tools.

01

Disconnected systems

Each piece of in-store equipment has its own supplier and panel; nobody sees the whole space.

02

Failures are noticed late

A screen that is off or background music that has stopped is discovered once it has already cost money.

03

Investing without testing

Without a replica of the space, every change is decided blind, before touching a cable.

02 · The solution

The experience goes beyond the screen.

Admira Store, with XpaceOS, puts the physical space at the centre: coordinate the content and systems that shape the in-store experience.

  • Content where it belongs

    Schedule assets by surface, location and time slot. Monitor dispatch and campaign distribution status.

  • A twin for every location

    Bring together locations, equipment and inventory to understand the network and manage each store’s experience.

  • A multisensory experience

    Coordinate signage, music and scent alongside Wi-Fi, security and IoT. Adapt communication to context.

FOR retailers, stores and operations teams.
