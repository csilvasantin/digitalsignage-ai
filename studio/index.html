01 · The challenge

Every screen asks for its own version.

A campaign in a physical space is not one asset: it is posters, menus, vertical videos, voice and music.

01

Many formats, one idea

Posters, digital menus, vertical videos and audio: every surface in the space needs its own adaptation.

02

Every adaptation, a new order

When every version depends on an outside supplier, content reaches the screen late.

03

The brand gets diluted

Across versions and formats, the logo, colours and tone get lost along the way.

02 · The solution

From a brief to an experience.

Admira Studio brings together AI content creation and adaptation to shape what your brand wants to say.

  • Choose how to tell your story

    Posters, digital menus and vertical videos for screens. Voice and music to complete your location’s experience.

  • One brief, many versions

    Define the message, format and duration. Use AI to create and adapt assets for each surface and campaign.

  • Your identity in every asset

    Your logo, colours and tone accompany the content, ready for distribution with Store.

FOR marketing teams, brands and creators.
