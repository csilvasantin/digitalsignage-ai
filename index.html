    02Field cases · open to inspect

    Two spaces you can walk into.

    Both are demonstrations, and both say so. Neither is presented as a customer deployment: no physical players are assigned and no audience figure is claimed.

    01Barcelona · Passeig de Gràcia 103

    Concept demo · built from public data

    A coffee shop, rebuilt as a twin.

    The Starbucks at Passeig de Gràcia 103, operated in Spain by Alsea, modelled from a 360° capture and public information. Six wall screens and the till displays run playlists and background music inside the twin. Unplug a screen there and a real incident opens in Yokup.

    • ShowsTwin, playlists, music and incident handling working together
    • ProductsXpaceOS · admira.app · admira.tv · Yokup
    • Not claimedA commercial relationship with Starbucks or Alsea, or playback on their hardware
    Open the store profile ↗

    02Cafebrería · prototype

    Prototype · no physical venue

    A café-bookshop where the shelf sells.

    A fictional café-bookshop built to test one idea: every object in the room can be a surface. Six books and six records on the shelf are interactive and link out to where they can be bought or played, and an operations layer tracks nineteen items in the room.

    • ShowsInteractive objects, a 3D room and an operations view in one model
    • ProductsXpaceOS · admira.app · Pixeria
    • Not claimedA real venue, assigned devices or live telemetry; item states are simulated

    04Entrances · one operating system

    Start with the work on your desk.

    The same network looks different depending on who has to run it. Choose the responsibility you carry today; each route names the problem, the working sequence and where to go next.

    01For the operator

    Keep the network legible after launch.

    Your problem is not publishing once. It is knowing what should be live, what actually played and what needs attention across every endpoint.

    1. 01

      Plan the content and endpoints that belong together.

    2. 02

      Publish one change across the intended network.

    3. 03

      Verify delivery and act on the exception.

    Open the operating layer →

    02For the integrator

    Connect the space without splitting the view.

    Screens, players and sensors may come from different systems. The job is to give them one observable handover instead of another isolated control panel.

    1. 01

      Map each endpoint and the signal it can expose.

    2. 02

      Connect content, context and response at the useful boundary.

    3. 03

      Hand over a network another team can inspect.

    Continue to connected endpoints →

    03For the creative team

    Design for a live context, not an export folder.

    A useful piece of content has a destination, a moment and a response. Build that logic into the brief before producing another set of disconnected formats.

    1. 01

      Compose the message and the condition that makes it relevant.

    2. 02

      Target the surfaces and interactions it belongs to.

    3. 03

      Learn from observable delivery, not assumed attention.

    Continue to content systems →

    04For the venue owner

    Define what the space should do first.

    Start with the visitor moment and the operational outcome. The feature list only becomes useful after the experience and its proof are clear.

    1. 01

      Name the moment in the space that needs to change.

    2. 02

      Choose the response people or systems should have.

    3. 03

      Define the evidence that would make the result reviewable.

    Continue to measurable evidence →

    01System · many identities
    
      
    
        
    Built to wear someone else’s name.
    
        
    One operating system for spaces, designed to recede behind the identity that people actually meet.
    
      
    
    
    
    
    
      
    
        
    This is not a logo swap on a slide. The brand is a runtime value: name, wordmark, domain and operator all resolve from one registry, so the same build serves two real editions today.
    
        
      
          
    • Nothing is hard-coded. Brand strings live in data-brand-* attributes, never inside copy — so switching cannot half-apply.
      • 
          
    • Switch by host or by query. Each edition has a shareable URL and resolves its own canonical origin.
      • 
          
    • The operator line is optional. Run it unattributed, or credit whoever operates the network.
      • 
        
    
        
    Choose an edition in the live desk. The page reloads the actual runtime, so the result can be copied, shared and checked without a simulated preview.
